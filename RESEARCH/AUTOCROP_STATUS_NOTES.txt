Status timeout / auto-crop update
=================================

- SDL3 status messages now expire automatically after 1.5 seconds.
- SDL1.2 status messages are routed through the window caption and restored to the emulator title after 1.5 seconds.
- The wasm status overlay now clears itself after 1.5 seconds.
- Added auto-crop for black borders in SDL1.2, SDL3, and wasm-clang.
- Auto-crop is enabled by default.
- SDL3 exposes it under System -> Auto-crop.
- wasm exposes an Auto-crop button in the top controls.
- The crop detector ignores tiny bright logos/fades and requires six stable frames before switching crop rectangles to avoid flicker.

Aggressive auto-crop update
===========================

Auto-crop now removes all detected solid/near-black image-edge borders.

Changes:
- Removed the previous 2-pixel crop guard band.
- Removed the previous rule that ignored 1-3 pixel borders around the image.
- Raised RGB near-black detection from <=6 to <=12 for 32-bit builds, so
  converted or dithered black borders are also treated as crop candidates.
- Kept the minimum visible-area safeguard and 6-frame stability hysteresis,
  so tiny logos/fades do not cause rapid zooming.

Applies to SDL1.2, SDL3, and wasm.
